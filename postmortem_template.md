## Summary

What failed? How were users affected? How was service restored?

## Impact

State affected operations, the time window, count, and denominator. Separate confirmed facts from estimates and unknowns.

## Timeline

Record timestamped events: first impact, detection, investigation, mitigation, and confirmed recovery. Include evidence behind important decisions.

## Causes and contributing conditions

Separate the trigger from the reasons it caused a failure. Explain behavior, missing safeguards, and testing or monitoring gaps. Avoid stopping at personal blame.

## What helped and what slowed recovery

Record useful signals, decisions, coordination, and tools. Note missing information or failed attempts and what they taught the team.

## Follow-up actions

For each action, record what will change, one owner, a due date, and the evidence needed to mark it complete. Choose concrete improvements to prevention, detection, mitigation, or recovery.

## Remaining work

Track uncertain payments, delayed work, data checks, customer follow-up, and unresolved questions. Mark the incident recovered separately from completing all improvement actions.
